Terms & Conditions

1. Purpose
This system is for official registration of WMSU Faculty Members only. By proceeding, you confirm that you are a legitimate faculty member of Western Mindanao State University.

2. Data Privacy
Your personal information, photo, and facial biometric data will be securely stored and used solely for identity verification and access control within the WMSU system. We do not share your data with third parties.

3. Accuracy
You agree to provide accurate and truthful information. Any falsification may result in denial of access or disciplinary action.

4. Facial Recognition
Your face will be scanned and encoded for secure login. This biometric data is encrypted and cannot be reverse-engineered into a photo.

5. Consent
By clicking "Accept", you consent to the collection and processing of your biometric and personal data as described.

Photo Guidelines

DO

  • Look straight at the camera
  • Even, bright lighting (no shadows on face)
  • Plain background, no other people
  • Head & shoulders only

DON'T

  • Side view / tilted head
  • Dark or harsh shadows
  • Other people in frame
  • Busy / patterned background

Additional Tips:

  • Use a neutral expression (no smile with teeth showing)
  • Remove hats, sunglasses, or anything covering the face
  • Take the photo in portrait orientation
  • File size less than or equal to 5 MB (JPG/PNG)
