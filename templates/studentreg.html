Terms & Conditions

1. Purpose
This system is exclusively for the official registration of currently enrolled students of Western Mindanao State University (WMSU). By proceeding, you confirm that you are a legitimate and active student of WMSU.

2. Data Privacy
Your personal information, student photo, and facial biometric data will be collected, securely stored, and used solely for identity verification, access control, and official university services within the WMSU system. We do not share your data with third parties except as required by law or official university purposes.

3. Accuracy of Information
You agree to provide complete, accurate, and truthful information during registration. Providing false information or impersonating another student may result in denial of access, cancellation of registration, or disciplinary action under the University’s Student Code of Conduct.

4. Facial Recognition and Biometric Data
Your face will be scanned and encoded to enable secure, passwordless login and attendance monitoring (where applicable). All biometric data is encrypted, stored securely, and cannot be reverse-engineered into a photograph.

5. Use of Data for University Services
You authorize WMSU to use your registered information and biometric data for official academic and administrative purposes, including but not limited to class attendance, library access, examination verification, and other campus services.

6. Consent
By clicking "Accept", you give your full and informed consent to the collection, processing, and use of your personal and biometric data as described above, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and university policies.

Photo Guidelines

DO

  • Look straight at the camera
  • Even, bright lighting (no shadows on face)
  • Plain background, no other people
  • Head & shoulders only

DON'T

  • Side view / tilted head
  • Dark or harsh shadows
  • Other people in frame
  • Busy / patterned background

Additional Tips:

  • Use a neutral expression (no smile with teeth showing)
  • Remove hats, sunglasses, or anything covering the face
  • Take the photo in portrait orientation
  • File size less than or equal to 5 MB (JPG/PNG)
